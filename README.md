# Diwali-Sales-Analysis

**Diwali Sales Analysis: Customer Purchase Behavior Insights**

## **Description**

This project analyzes Diwali season sales data to uncover customer purchasing patterns and key factors influencing sales performance. Through data cleaning, visualization, and descriptive analysis, the notebook aims to derive actionable business insights that can help improve marketing, inventory, and customer targeting strategies.

## **Purpose**

The main goal is to explore and understand customer behavior during the Diwali festive season.
Specifically:

* Identify the demographic groups contributing most to sales.
* Analyze top-performing states, occupations, and product categories.
* Provide data-driven recommendations for targeted marketing and inventory planning during future festive campaigns.

## **Tech Stack**

* **Python**

  * `pandas` — for data manipulation and cleaning
  * `numpy` — for numerical operations
  * `matplotlib` & `seaborn` — for data visualization and trend analysis
* **Jupyter Notebook** — for interactive analysis and reporting

## **Data Source**

The dataset (likely a `.csv` file) contains **Diwali sales records** with columns such as:

* Customer details: Gender, Age Group, Marital Status, Occupation
* Location details: State, Zone
* Purchase details: Product Category, Amount, Orders

## **Features / Highlights**

1. **Data Cleaning:** Removed irrelevant and null columns, standardized column names, and converted data types.
2. **Demographic Insights:**

   * Majority of buyers were **females aged 26–35**.
   * Married women showed higher purchasing power.
3. **Geographic Insights:**

   * Top-performing states: **Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, Karnataka**.
4. **Occupational Insights:**

   * High sales among **IT, Healthcare, and Aviation** professionals.
5. **Product Insights:**

   * Certain product categories had significantly higher demand during Diwali.


## **Business Problem**

During the Diwali festival, e-commerce and retail businesses experience a sales surge, but often lack clear understanding of:

* Which customer segments to target
* What products to promote
* Which regions to prioritize

Without data-backed insights, marketing budgets can be wasted, and inventory can become unbalanced (overstocking or stockouts).


## **Business Impact**

The analysis provides:

* **Targeted Marketing:** Helps focus campaigns on high-value demographics (e.g., married females aged 26–35).
* **Inventory Optimization:** Guides product stocking decisions based on state and occupation-based demand trends.

## Visual Insights
The notebook includes visualizations for:
- Gender vs. Total Orders & Amount
- Age Group vs. Purchase Amount
- Top 10 States by Orders & Sales
- Occupation and Product Category Analysis

---

##  Conclusion
This analysis helps identify that **married females aged 26–35 from top states like Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Karnataka**
are the most valuable customers during the Diwali season. Businesses can leverage these insights for **personalized marketing,
inventory planning, and sales forecasting**.
